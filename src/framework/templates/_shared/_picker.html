{# _picker(options) — renders a "choose your path" card grid. Two species, one component (full contract: the picker entry in `framework/templates/README.md`, which extends RESOURCE_GRAMMAR.md): a *discriminator/type picker* funnels to a kind-/type-specific sub-form of the SAME resource via a selecting query param (`/posts/form?kind=…`, `/organizations/form?type=…`); a *dispatching picker* points its cards OUT at OTHER resources' create forms (`/profile/identity` → `/clinicians/form` + `/organizations/form`).
