The icons come from `CLIENT_REFERRAL_SERVICE_ICONS` and `TREATMENT_SETTINGS_ICONS` in `src/domain/models/enums.py`; the Lucide font CSS in `base.html` renders the glyphs. Icons next to a labeled item are unambiguous — the reader sees "pill | Medication management" together — unlike the earlier icon-only experiment. Right column ("Contact") names *who* you're reaching and gives a clickable `mailto:` link as the action: * `client_referral` → `{username}` (the referring provider — the User account is the only identity we have for them; rendered as plain text, matching the bare-name style PA uses for the practice) + ``. * `provider_availability` → `{practice_name}` (the practice IS the provider; the practice_name is also in the header but repeating it here keeps the email anchored to a name at scan distance) + ``.
